# SKU-012 Sodium Chloride USP
Summary: SKU-012 Sodium Chloride USP: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-02.

Facts:
- SKU-012 (Sodium Chloride USP) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-012 (Sodium Chloride USP) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-012 (Sodium Chloride USP) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-012 (Sodium Chloride USP) is supplied by SUP-02 (Northgate Chemicals).
- SKU-012 (Sodium Chloride USP) has a reorder point of 120 units.

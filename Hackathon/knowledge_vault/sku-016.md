# SKU-016 Silicon Dioxide
Summary: SKU-016 Silicon Dioxide: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-016 (Silicon Dioxide) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-016 (Silicon Dioxide) has a shelf life of 365 days.
- SKU-016 (Silicon Dioxide) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-016 (Silicon Dioxide) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-016 (Silicon Dioxide) has a reorder point of 40 units.

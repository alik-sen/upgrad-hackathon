# SKU-025 Phosphoric Acid 85%
Summary: SKU-025 Phosphoric Acid 85%: corrosive product stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-025 (Phosphoric Acid 85%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- SKU-025 (Phosphoric Acid 85%) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-025 (Phosphoric Acid 85%) has hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- SKU-025 (Phosphoric Acid 85%) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-025 (Phosphoric Acid 85%) has a reorder point of 40 units.

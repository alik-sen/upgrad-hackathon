# SOP-RK-08 Product Recall
Summary: A Class I recall must be notified to the regulator within 4 hours.

Facts:
- A Class I recall must be notified to the regulator within 4 hours.
- A Class II recall must be notified within 24 hours.
- A recall effectiveness check must be completed at 30 days.

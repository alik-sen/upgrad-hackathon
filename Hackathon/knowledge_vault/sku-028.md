# SKU-028 IV Catheter 20G
Summary: SKU-028 IV Catheter 20G: sterile-device product stored at 15-30°C in sealed packaging, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-028 (IV Catheter 20G) must be stored at 15-30°C in sealed packaging.
- SKU-028 (IV Catheter 20G) has a shelf life of 540 days.
- SKU-028 (IV Catheter 20G) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-028 (IV Catheter 20G) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-028 (IV Catheter 20G) has a reorder point of 40 units.

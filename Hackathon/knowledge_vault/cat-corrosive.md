# Category: Corrosive Reagents
Summary: Corrosive Reagents category governed by sop-hazmat.

Facts:
- Corrosive Reagents products are stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- Corrosive Reagents products fall under hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- Handling of Corrosive Reagents products is governed by SOP-HZ-04 (sop-hazmat).

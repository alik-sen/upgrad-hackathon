# SKU-003 Insulin Glargine Cartridge
Summary: SKU-003 Insulin Glargine Cartridge: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-003 (Insulin Glargine Cartridge) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-003 (Insulin Glargine Cartridge) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-003 (Insulin Glargine Cartridge) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-003 (Insulin Glargine Cartridge) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-003 (Insulin Glargine Cartridge) has a reorder point of 200 units.

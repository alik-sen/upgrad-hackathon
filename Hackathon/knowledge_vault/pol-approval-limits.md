# POL-01 Purchase Approval Limits
Summary: Purchases up to $5,000 can be approved by a supervisor.

Facts:
- Purchases up to $5,000 can be approved by a supervisor.
- Purchases above $5,000 and up to $50,000 require department director approval.
- Purchases above $50,000 require CFO approval.

# SKU-014 Croscarmellose Sodium
Summary: SKU-014 Croscarmellose Sodium: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-03.

Facts:
- SKU-014 (Croscarmellose Sodium) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-014 (Croscarmellose Sodium) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-014 (Croscarmellose Sodium) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-014 (Croscarmellose Sodium) is supplied by SUP-03 (Brightwater Pharma Supply).
- SKU-014 (Croscarmellose Sodium) has a reorder point of 60 units.

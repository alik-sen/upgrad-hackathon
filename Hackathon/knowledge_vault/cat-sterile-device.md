# Category: Sterile Devices
Summary: Sterile Devices category governed by sop-receiving.

Facts:
- Sterile Devices products are stored at 15-30°C in sealed packaging.
- Sterile Devices products fall under hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- Handling of Sterile Devices products is governed by SOP-RC-03 (sop-receiving).

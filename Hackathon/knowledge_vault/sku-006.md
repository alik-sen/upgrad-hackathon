# SKU-006 Enzyme Reagent Kit
Summary: SKU-006 Enzyme Reagent Kit: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-006 (Enzyme Reagent Kit) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-006 (Enzyme Reagent Kit) has a shelf life of 540 days.
- SKU-006 (Enzyme Reagent Kit) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-006 (Enzyme Reagent Kit) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-006 (Enzyme Reagent Kit) has a reorder point of 80 units.

# SOP-SA-05 Supplier Audit Program
Summary: Tier A suppliers must be audited every 24 months.

Facts:
- Tier A suppliers must be audited every 24 months.
- Tier B suppliers must be audited every 12 months.
- Tier C suppliers must be audited every 6 months.
- The supplier audit report must be issued within 30 days of the audit.

# SKU-018 Acetonitrile HPLC Grade
Summary: SKU-018 Acetonitrile HPLC Grade: flammable product stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet, supplier SUP-06.

Facts:
- SKU-018 (Acetonitrile HPLC Grade) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- SKU-018 (Acetonitrile HPLC Grade) has a shelf life of 540 days.
- SKU-018 (Acetonitrile HPLC Grade) has hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- SKU-018 (Acetonitrile HPLC Grade) is supplied by SUP-06 (Evermoor Packaging Systems).
- SKU-018 (Acetonitrile HPLC Grade) has a reorder point of 350 units.

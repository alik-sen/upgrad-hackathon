# SKU-007 Hyaluronidase Vial
Summary: SKU-007 Hyaluronidase Vial: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-05.

Facts:
- SKU-007 (Hyaluronidase Vial) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-007 (Hyaluronidase Vial) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-007 (Hyaluronidase Vial) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-007 (Hyaluronidase Vial) is supplied by SUP-05 (Delmar Industrial Reagents).
- SKU-007 (Hyaluronidase Vial) has a reorder point of 350 units.

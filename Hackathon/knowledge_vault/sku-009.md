# SKU-009 Microcrystalline Cellulose
Summary: SKU-009 Microcrystalline Cellulose: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-06.

Facts:
- SKU-009 (Microcrystalline Cellulose) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-009 (Microcrystalline Cellulose) has a shelf life of 270 days.
- SKU-009 (Microcrystalline Cellulose) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-009 (Microcrystalline Cellulose) is supplied by SUP-06 (Evermoor Packaging Systems).
- SKU-009 (Microcrystalline Cellulose) has a reorder point of 20 units.

# SKU-010 Lactose Monohydrate
Summary: SKU-010 Lactose Monohydrate: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-05.

Facts:
- SKU-010 (Lactose Monohydrate) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-010 (Lactose Monohydrate) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-010 (Lactose Monohydrate) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-010 (Lactose Monohydrate) is supplied by SUP-05 (Delmar Industrial Reagents).
- SKU-010 (Lactose Monohydrate) has a reorder point of 350 units.

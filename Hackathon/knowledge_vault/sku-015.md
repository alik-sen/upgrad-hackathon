# SKU-015 Talc Powder
Summary: SKU-015 Talc Powder: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-05.

Facts:
- SKU-015 (Talc Powder) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-015 (Talc Powder) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-015 (Talc Powder) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-015 (Talc Powder) is supplied by SUP-05 (Delmar Industrial Reagents).
- SKU-015 (Talc Powder) has a reorder point of 80 units.

# SKU-004 Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion
Summary: SKU-004 Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-004 (Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-004 (Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-004 (Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-004 (Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-004 (Vaccine Adjuvant Emulsion) has a reorder point of 80 units.

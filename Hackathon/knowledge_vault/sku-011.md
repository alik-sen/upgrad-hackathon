# SKU-011 Magnesium Stearate
Summary: SKU-011 Magnesium Stearate: ambient product stored at 15-25°C, supplier SUP-02.

Facts:
- SKU-011 (Magnesium Stearate) must be stored at 15-25°C.
- SKU-011 (Magnesium Stearate) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-011 (Magnesium Stearate) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-011 (Magnesium Stearate) is supplied by SUP-02 (Northgate Chemicals).
- SKU-011 (Magnesium Stearate) has a reorder point of 20 units.

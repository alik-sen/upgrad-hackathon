# SKU-024 Sulfuric Acid 96%
Summary: SKU-024 Sulfuric Acid 96%: corrosive product stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet, supplier SUP-04.

Facts:
- SKU-024 (Sulfuric Acid 96%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- SKU-024 (Sulfuric Acid 96%) has a shelf life of 1095 days.
- SKU-024 (Sulfuric Acid 96%) has hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- SKU-024 (Sulfuric Acid 96%) is supplied by SUP-04 (Corvid Laboratory Products).
- SKU-024 (Sulfuric Acid 96%) has a reorder point of 120 units.

# POL-04 Incident Escalation SLAs
Summary: A P1 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.

Facts:
- A P1 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
- A P2 incident must be acknowledged within 4 hours.
- A P3 incident must be acknowledged within 2 business days.

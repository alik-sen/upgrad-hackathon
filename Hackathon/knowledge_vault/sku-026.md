# SKU-026 Nitric Acid 65%
Summary: SKU-026 Nitric Acid 65%: corrosive product stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet, supplier SUP-04.

Facts:
- SKU-026 (Nitric Acid 65%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- SKU-026 (Nitric Acid 65%) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-026 (Nitric Acid 65%) has hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- SKU-026 (Nitric Acid 65%) is supplied by SUP-04 (Corvid Laboratory Products).
- SKU-026 (Nitric Acid 65%) has a reorder point of 60 units.

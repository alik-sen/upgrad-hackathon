# SKU-021 Acetone Technical
Summary: SKU-021 Acetone Technical: flammable product stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet, supplier SUP-06.

Facts:
- SKU-021 (Acetone Technical) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- SKU-021 (Acetone Technical) has a shelf life of 270 days.
- SKU-021 (Acetone Technical) has hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- SKU-021 (Acetone Technical) is supplied by SUP-06 (Evermoor Packaging Systems).
- SKU-021 (Acetone Technical) has a reorder point of 80 units.

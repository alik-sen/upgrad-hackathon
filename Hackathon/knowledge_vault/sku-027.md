# SKU-027 Sterile Syringe 10 mL
Summary: SKU-027 Sterile Syringe 10 mL: sterile-device product stored at 15-30°C in sealed packaging, supplier SUP-02.

Facts:
- SKU-027 (Sterile Syringe 10 mL) must be stored at 15-30°C in sealed packaging.
- SKU-027 (Sterile Syringe 10 mL) has a shelf life of 365 days.
- SKU-027 (Sterile Syringe 10 mL) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-027 (Sterile Syringe 10 mL) is supplied by SUP-02 (Northgate Chemicals).
- SKU-027 (Sterile Syringe 10 mL) has a reorder point of 500 units.

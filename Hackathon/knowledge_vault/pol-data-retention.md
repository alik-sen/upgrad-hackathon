# POL-02 Records and Data Retention
Summary: Batch records must be retained for 7 years.

Facts:
- Batch records must be retained for 7 years.
- Audit logs must be retained for 10 years.
- Chat transcripts must be deleted after 30 days.
- Personally identifiable information must never be stored in raw form.

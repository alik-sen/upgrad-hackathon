# SKU-019 Ethanol Absolute
Summary: SKU-019 Ethanol Absolute: flammable product stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet, supplier SUP-04.

Facts:
- SKU-019 (Ethanol Absolute) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- SKU-019 (Ethanol Absolute) has a shelf life of 365 days.
- SKU-019 (Ethanol Absolute) has hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- SKU-019 (Ethanol Absolute) is supplied by SUP-04 (Corvid Laboratory Products).
- SKU-019 (Ethanol Absolute) has a reorder point of 500 units.

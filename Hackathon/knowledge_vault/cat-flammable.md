# Category: Flammable Solvents
Summary: Flammable Solvents category governed by sop-hazmat.

Facts:
- Flammable Solvents products are stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- Flammable Solvents products fall under hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- Handling of Flammable Solvents products is governed by SOP-HZ-04 (sop-hazmat).

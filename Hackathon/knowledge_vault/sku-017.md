# SKU-017 Isopropyl Alcohol 99%
Summary: SKU-017 Isopropyl Alcohol 99%: flammable product stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-017 (Isopropyl Alcohol 99%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- SKU-017 (Isopropyl Alcohol 99%) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-017 (Isopropyl Alcohol 99%) has hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- SKU-017 (Isopropyl Alcohol 99%) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-017 (Isopropyl Alcohol 99%) has a reorder point of 80 units.

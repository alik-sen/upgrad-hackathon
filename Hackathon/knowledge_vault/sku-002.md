# SKU-002 Monoclonal Antibody Vial
Summary: SKU-002 Monoclonal Antibody Vial: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-002 (Monoclonal Antibody Vial) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-002 (Monoclonal Antibody Vial) has a shelf life of 1095 days.
- SKU-002 (Monoclonal Antibody Vial) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-002 (Monoclonal Antibody Vial) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-002 (Monoclonal Antibody Vial) has a reorder point of 40 units.

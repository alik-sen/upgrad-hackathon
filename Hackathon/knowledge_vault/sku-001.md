# SKU-001 Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution
Summary: SKU-001 Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-02.

Facts:
- SKU-001 (Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-001 (Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution) has a shelf life of 365 days.
- SKU-001 (Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-001 (Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution) is supplied by SUP-02 (Northgate Chemicals).
- SKU-001 (Recombinant Albumin 5% Solution) has a reorder point of 350 units.

# SKU-022 Hydrochloric Acid 37%
Summary: SKU-022 Hydrochloric Acid 37%: corrosive product stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet, supplier SUP-05.

Facts:
- SKU-022 (Hydrochloric Acid 37%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- SKU-022 (Hydrochloric Acid 37%) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-022 (Hydrochloric Acid 37%) has hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- SKU-022 (Hydrochloric Acid 37%) is supplied by SUP-05 (Delmar Industrial Reagents).
- SKU-022 (Hydrochloric Acid 37%) has a reorder point of 120 units.

# SKU-008 Growth Factor Concentrate
Summary: SKU-008 Growth Factor Concentrate: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-05.

Facts:
- SKU-008 (Growth Factor Concentrate) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-008 (Growth Factor Concentrate) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-008 (Growth Factor Concentrate) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-008 (Growth Factor Concentrate) is supplied by SUP-05 (Delmar Industrial Reagents).
- SKU-008 (Growth Factor Concentrate) has a reorder point of 40 units.

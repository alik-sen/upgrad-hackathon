# SKU-005 Plasma Protein Fraction
Summary: SKU-005 Plasma Protein Fraction: cold-chain product stored at 2-8°C, supplier SUP-01.

Facts:
- SKU-005 (Plasma Protein Fraction) must be stored at 2-8°C.
- SKU-005 (Plasma Protein Fraction) has a shelf life of 180 days.
- SKU-005 (Plasma Protein Fraction) has hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- SKU-005 (Plasma Protein Fraction) is supplied by SUP-01 (Aldervale Biosciences).
- SKU-005 (Plasma Protein Fraction) has a reorder point of 350 units.

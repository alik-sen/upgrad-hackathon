# SKU-023 Sodium Hydroxide 50%
Summary: SKU-023 Sodium Hydroxide 50%: corrosive product stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet, supplier SUP-04.

Facts:
- SKU-023 (Sodium Hydroxide 50%) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a corrosives cabinet.
- SKU-023 (Sodium Hydroxide 50%) has a shelf life of 730 days.
- SKU-023 (Sodium Hydroxide 50%) has hazard classification Class 8 (corrosive).
- SKU-023 (Sodium Hydroxide 50%) is supplied by SUP-04 (Corvid Laboratory Products).
- SKU-023 (Sodium Hydroxide 50%) has a reorder point of 200 units.

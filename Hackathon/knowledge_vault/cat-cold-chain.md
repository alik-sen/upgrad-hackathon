# Category: Cold-Chain Biologics
Summary: Cold-Chain Biologics category governed by sop-cold-chain.

Facts:
- Cold-Chain Biologics products are stored at 2-8°C.
- Cold-Chain Biologics products fall under hazard classification Class 0 (non-hazardous).
- Handling of Cold-Chain Biologics products is governed by SOP-CC-01 (sop-cold-chain).

# SKU-020 Methanol ACS
Summary: SKU-020 Methanol ACS: flammable product stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet, supplier SUP-03.

Facts:
- SKU-020 (Methanol ACS) must be stored at 15-25°C inside a flammable-rated cabinet.
- SKU-020 (Methanol ACS) has a shelf life of 365 days.
- SKU-020 (Methanol ACS) has hazard classification Class 3 (flammable liquid).
- SKU-020 (Methanol ACS) is supplied by SUP-03 (Brightwater Pharma Supply).
- SKU-020 (Methanol ACS) has a reorder point of 80 units.
